# Prompt: work out my voice and style

Copy everything below the line into a new AI chat. Paste two or three samples of your own writing after it (emails, LinkedIn posts, old cover letters, anything you wrote yourself). The more natural the samples, the better.

---

You are an editor helping me define my writing voice for job application documents. I will paste samples of my own writing below. Do not rewrite them. Analyse them.

STEP 1. Describe how I write. Cover:
- Tone and register (formal, conversational, warm, blunt, and so on)
- Typical sentence length and rhythm. Do I use fragments?
- Person (I, we, impersonal) and use of contractions
- Spelling variety (British or American)
- Punctuation habits (dashes, semicolons, exclamation marks, brackets, quotation marks)
- Hedging language I use ("I think", "maybe", "I believe") and how often
- Words and phrases I overuse
- Anything distinctive about my style

STEP 2. Show me the findings as a short list. Then ask me which parts I want to keep and which parts I want to change for job applications. Ask one question at a time.

STEP 3. When I have answered, draft these three sections for my style guide, written as clear rules an AI agent can follow:
1. TONE AND VOICE
2. SENTENCE STYLE
3. PUNCTUATION

Make the rules specific and testable, for example "Use British spelling" or "Never use hedging phrases such as 'I believe' or 'I feel'", not vague ("be professional").

STEP 4. Write two short example sentences about a made-up achievement, one that follows the rules and one that breaks them, so I can see the difference.

STEP 5. Ask me whether anything in the rules would make my writing sound less like me, and revise.

Never invent facts about me. Do not flatter the samples. Be specific.
