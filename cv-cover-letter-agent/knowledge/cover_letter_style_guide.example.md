# Cover Letter Style Guide (TEMPLATE)

> **HOW TO USE THIS FILE**
> 1. Copy this file to `cover_letter_style_guide.md` (same folder, without `.example`).
> 2. Read every section. Lines marked **REVIEW** are default choices that may not suit you. Change them using `docs/03-set-your-voice-and-style.md`.
> 3. Pay special attention to **Structure**. It decides how every letter is built, so reshape it until it matches how you want to present yourself.
> 4. When you have finished, delete `(TEMPLATE)` from the title line above and delete this box.

This file defines the tone, structure and rules for all cover letters written on your behalf. It works alongside the CV Style Guide, which is the source of truth for career history, figures and achievements.

## Tone

**REVIEW: change this to the voice you want.**

- Direct, confident, conviction-led. State things as fact, backed by real achievements. No hedging ("I believe", "I feel", "I think I could").
- No false modesty and no unsupported bravado. Confidence is earned through the work described.
- Authoritative but natural: the way a confident person would say it out loud, not stiff corporate prose.
- Spelling: **[BRITISH or AMERICAN]**.

## Sentence style

**REVIEW:**

- Mix medium-length flowing sentences (connecting an achievement to its relevance: "I did X, which meant Y, directly relevant to Z") with short declarative sentences and deliberate fragments for rhythm.
- Direct address to the reader is acceptable where it helps, without overuse.

## Punctuation

**REVIEW:**

- Use hyphens normally in compound words (multi-site, end-to-end).
- Avoid em dashes and en dashes as a device for asides or dramatic pauses. Use commas, full stops or separate sentences.

## Structure

**REVIEW: this is the structure every letter follows. Add, remove or reorder parts to suit you, and keep the numbered list below in sync with your choices.**

1. **Opening line:** a genuine, specific reason for applying, tied to the role. Match the company's register: measured for formal employers, more energetic for startups. Never generic enthusiasm.
2. **Why this company paragraph:** a dedicated paragraph on why you want to work at this specific business. Reference something specific the company does or has achieved, written as a personal reaction, not a recitation of company facts. If research has not been done, insert this placeholder and flag it before finalising:
   > `[PLACEHOLDER: Insert specific detail about what this company does, has achieved, or its mission, researched from their website, recent news, or job description. Do not use generic praise.]`
3. **Cross-referencing:** in at least one experience paragraph, explicitly tie an achievement back to what was said about the company.
4. **Experience paragraphs:** two or three, drawn from the Career History. Each is framed as an achievement: what you did, how you did it, what happened as a result, with real figures where recorded. If an experience does not honestly fit a requirement, say so to the user and ask for better context rather than forcing a weak link.
5. **Closing paragraph:** brief, confident, inviting further discussion. No sign-off in the body.

## Handling gaps in experience

**REVIEW:**

- Never call out or apologise for gaps ("I don't have X, but...").
- Reframe around the closest genuine transferable experience.
- Never fabricate direct experience.

## Numbers and specifics

Use real figures exactly as recorded in the Career History. Do not soften them into vague language.

## Salary

Never include salary or compensation expectations.

## Sign-off

The sign-off and your name are added automatically by the document generator. Never write a closing salutation in the letter body. To change "Kind regards", edit `tailor_application.py`.

## Contact details

Handled by the document template and `profile.json`. The agent never asks for them.

## Length

**REVIEW:** exactly one page. Prioritise the two or three strongest, most relevant achievements.

## Keyword alignment (secondary to the CV)

Echo job-spec language where truthful and natural. The CV carries the primary ATS weight.

## Format defaults

- Name in standard title case, never all capitals.
- One page, single column, no images, tables, headers or footers.
- No markdown syntax, symbols or emoji in the letter.
- Output as a Word document (.docx).
