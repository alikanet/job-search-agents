## Fictional worked example

## 99. Warehouse system migration
**Fabrikam Retail, Programme Manager, Mar 2019 to Nov 2021**

- **Headline:** Led a warehouse system migration across 9 distribution centres, finishing on budget and cutting order-picking errors by 20%.
- **Situation:** Each site ran its own processes and the old system could not scale to peak season.
- **Action (recorded):** Piloted at 2 sites, then phased the remaining 7 over 10 months, with a weekly steering group of finance, operations and IT.
- **Result:** All 9 sites live on budget, order-picking errors down 20%.
- **Hardest moment:** One shift supervisor resisted the new handheld scanners. Ran early-morning drop-in sessions until the team trusted the new process.
- **[TO ADD]:** What the pilot sites taught you that changed the rollout plan.
- **Guardrails:** Say "led the programme", not "built the system". Do not claim technical depth in the warehouse software itself.
